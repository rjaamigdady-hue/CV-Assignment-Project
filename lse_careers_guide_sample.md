## Extract Section 1: UK CV Length & Structure
In the UK, a standard graduate CV should generally not exceed two pages. You should never include personal details such as your photograph, date of birth, age, marital status, or nationality unless specifically requested by an international employer. Focus on contact details: full name, professional email address, phone number, and a link to your LinkedIn profile or personal portfolio.

## Extract Section 2: Reverse Chronological Order
Always present your Education and Work Experience sections in reverse chronological order, placing your most recent degree or employment entry at the top. Clearly state your university name, degree title, expected graduation year, and key relevant modules.

## Extract Section 3: Tailoring for Technical Roles
Tailor your CV for each specific position by extracting keywords from the job description. Highlight relevant quantitative skills, programming tools, and technical methodologies within the first page of your CV to maximize visibility during initial employer screening.
