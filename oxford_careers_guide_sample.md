## Extract Section 1: Describing Technical Projects
When showcasing academic or graduation projects, focus on demonstrating problem-solving capabilities and actionable outcomes. Structure bullet points logically: state the problem context, highlight the specific tools and algorithms implemented (e.g., Python, SQL, machine learning models), and conclude with measurable outputs or performance metrics.

## Extract Section 2: Technical & Soft Skills Presentation
Categorize technical proficiency clearly under a designated 'Technical Skills' section, grouped by programming languages, frameworks, databases, and specialized software. Soft skills (such as communication or teamwork) should not be listed as isolated adjectives; instead, demonstrate them through context within project and leadership descriptions.

## Extract Section 3: Formatting & ATS Compatibility
Ensure your document utilizes clean, standard typography (e.g., Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, 10–12pt) with consistent margins. Avoid multi-column tables, floating text boxes, and embedded visual graphics to maintain readability across Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
