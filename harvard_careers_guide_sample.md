**Institution:** Harvard University  
**Document:** Undergraduate Resumes and Cover Letters Guide  
**Target Region:** US Standard  

---

## Extract Section 1: The One-Page Resume Standard
For undergraduate students and recent graduates applying within the US market, a resume must strictly adhere to a single-page limit. Conciseness is paramount. Prioritize recent, high-impact experiences and omit secondary school details after completing your sophomore year.

## Extract Section 2: Strong Action Verbs & Impact Quantification
Begin every bullet point with a powerful action verb written in the past tense for completed positions (e.g., *Engineered*, *Optimized*, *Developed*, *Analyzed*) and in the present tense for ongoing roles. Quantify accomplishments wherever possible using specific numbers, percentages, or scale indicators (e.g., "Optimized query runtime by 35% across a 100k-row database").

## Extract Section 3: Action-Context-Result Framework (STAR)
Structure achievements to reflect the Situation/Task, Action, and Result. Ensure that the business or technical outcome of your work is explicitly stated rather than merely listing routine responsibilities.
